Privacy policy

Star Notes for iPhone, by Asadullah Siddiqi. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Star Notes collects no data

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you write is sent to me or to anyone else.

Where your notes live

Your notes are files on your iPhone. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive, they are saved there instead, and Apple stores and syncs them under Apple's privacy policy. Star Notes has no server, so your notes never pass through one.

Deleting your notes

Deleting the app removes the notes kept on the phone. Notes in an iCloud Drive folder stay there until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: asadullah@live.dk.
