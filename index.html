How do I see my notes on my other devices?

Open Library, find Vault and tap Connect, then pick a folder in iCloud Drive. Your notes are saved there as plain Markdown files, so any device or Markdown editor that can open that folder can read them.

Where are my notes kept?

Until you connect a folder, they stay on your iPhone only. Star Notes has no account and no server of its own.

How do I get a deleted note back?

Open Trash and tap Put back on the note.

How do collections work?

A note sorts itself by its first words: "watch Dune" goes to Watch list, "buy milk" to Shopping. Move a note to another collection and the next one like it follows.
